Projects · 01

Computer vision · iOS · live on device

Basketball Shot Tracker

Watches basketball video from a phone on a tripod, finds every shot and calls it made or missed with the reason. I trained a YOLOv8 ball-and-hoop detector and wrote the ball tracker and shot judge (arc fit, rim crossing, rim and net checks) from scratch.

Scored 85.6% end-to-end on 90 blind-labelled shots in a gym the system had never seen, with the code frozen and fingerprinted before a single scoring run. It found 87 of 90 shots and invented none.

Then ported it to a native Swift iPhone app (SwiftUI, Core ML on the Neural Engine) that runs live at ~30 detections per second and is verified to match the Python system exactly.

Projects · 02

Machine learning · web app

PulseCheck

End-to-end heart-disease risk prediction: cleaned a real multi-hospital dataset, compared three models and served the winner as a Flask web app with a 4-step assessment, per-prediction explanations and private per-visitor history.

Tuned for the metric that matters: 99% recall (101 of 102 sick patients caught) on a held-out test set of 184, ROC-AUC 0.90. Moving the cutoff from 0.50 to 0.18 cut missed patients from 13 to 1.

Calibrated the probabilities so "18% risk" actually means 18%, and packaged everything with Docker.

  • scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • Pandas

Projects · 03

Capstone · Android · on-device ML

Drowsy Driver Detection

A real-time Android app that watches the driver through the front camera and sounds an alarm when fatigue builds up, tracking eye closure, yawning and head tilt.

MediaPipe face landmarks drive eye and mouth aspect ratios, and two MobileNetV2 models (TensorFlow Lite) classify cropped eyes and mouths. They were trained on a custom 16,000-image dataset we collected with our own labelling tool: 99.17% accuracy on eye state and 96.18% on yawns.

A weighted multi-cue score keeps alerts stable even when one cue struggles (glasses, dim light). Built with a team of four for the TMU engineering capstone.

  • Kotlin
  • Android
  • CameraX
  • MediaPipe
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • MobileNetV2
